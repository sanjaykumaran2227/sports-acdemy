About Our Academy

Founded with a vision to nurture grassroots talent into world-class professional athletes, Elite Cricket Academy offers holistic development programs encompassing high-performance tactical training, data-driven analysis, and customized fitness regimes.

Our curriculum is designed by ICC-certified coaches ensuring every student receives tailored attention to hone their specific natural strengths.

Our Coaching Programs

Batting

Learn batting techniques, footwork, shot selection, timing, and match-winning skills from experienced coaches.

Bowling

Master fast bowling, swing, spin, yorkers, line and length, and bowling strategies for all formats.

Fielding

Improve catching, throwing accuracy, diving, reflexes, and overall fitness to become an excellent fielder.
